# Business Books & Online Resources

## Overview
A curated catalog of freely available online books and resources covering business models, operations, and end-to-end business management. These can be ingested into the knowledge base or referenced on demand.

## How to Use This Catalog

### On-Demand Reference
The knowledge router can load any of these resources when an agent needs specific business knowledge.

### Ingestion
For deeper integration, books can be:
1. Downloaded (if freely available)
2. Parsed and chunked
3. Added to the knowledge base
4. Made available for semantic search

### Priority Levels
- **P0**: Essential - Always include in knowledge base
- **P1**: Recommended - Ingest for key topics
- **P2**: Optional - Reference when needed

## Knowledge Ingestion Strategy

### Recommended Books for Full Ingestion (P0)

For a comprehensive business knowledge base, prioritize ingesting:

1. **Business Model Generation** - Osterwalder (Business models)
2. **The Lean Startup** - Ries (Lean methodology)
3. **Running Lean** - Maurya (Lean Canvas)
4. **The Four Steps to the Epiphany** - Blank (Customer Development)
5. **Disciplined Entrepreneurship** - Aulet (24 steps)
6. **High Output Management** - Grove (Management)
7. **The Effective Executive** - Drucker (Effectiveness)
8. **Principles** - Dalio (Principles)
9. **Hooked** - Eyal (Habit formation)
10. **Traction** - Weinberg (Growth channels)
11. **Obviously Awesome** - Dunford (Positioning)
12. **Sprint** - Knapp (Design sprints)
13. **Measure What Matters** - Doerr (OKRs)
14. **Customer Success** - Mehta (CS methodology)
15. **Product-Led Growth** - Bush (PLG)
16. **Site Reliability Engineering** - Google (SRE)
17. **Lean Analytics** - Croll (Analytics)
18. **Don't Make Me Think** - Krug (Usability)
19. **The Lean Product Playbook** - Olsen (Product)

### Ingestion Process

1. **Download** the book (if freely available)
2. **Parse** into chapters/sections
3. **Chunk** into manageable pieces (~2000 tokens each)
4. **Extract** key concepts, frameworks, examples
5. **Tag** with topics, frameworks, business types
6. **Index** for semantic search
7. **Integrate** with knowledge router

### On-Demand Reference

For resources not ingested, the knowledge router can:
- Link to the resource
- Provide a summary
- Extract relevant sections when needed
- Reference the source for deeper reading
