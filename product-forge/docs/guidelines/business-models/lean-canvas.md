# Lean Canvas

## Overview
The Lean Canvas is a 1-page business plan template adapted from the Business Model Canvas by Ash Maurya. It's optimized for startups and focuses on problems, solutions, and key metrics.

## The 9 Lean Canvas Blocks

### 1. Problem
Top 1-3 problems your target customers face.
- **Format**: List of problems
- **Example**: "Small businesses can't afford enterprise software"
- **Tips**:
  - Be specific about the problem
  - Validate through customer interviews
  - Identify severity and frequency

### 2. Customer Segments
Who are your target customers?
- **Format**: List of customer segments
- **Example**: "Small business owners, 10-50 employees"
- **Tips**:
  - Be specific (not "everyone")
  - Identify earlyvangelists
  - Map to problem

### 3. Unique Value Proposition (UVP)
The single most compelling benefit.
- **Format**: One clear statement
- **Formula**: "We help [customer] do [value] by [solution]"
- **Example**: "We help small businesses manage their finances through simple, affordable software"
- **Tips**:
  - Clear and concise
  - Focus on benefit, not feature
  - Test and iterate

### 4. Solution
Top 1-3 features that solve the problem.
- **Format**: List of solution approaches
- **Example**: "Mobile-first accounting, automated categorization"
- **Tips**:
  - Start simple
  - Validate before building
  - Map to problems

### 5. Channels
How will you reach customers?
- **Format**: List of channels
- **Example**: "Direct sales, content marketing, partnerships"
- **Tips**:
  - Start with what works
  - Test new channels
  - Optimize over time

### 6. Revenue Streams
How will you make money?
- **Format**: Pricing model and expected revenue
- **Example**: "$50/month subscription, 1000 customers = $50K MRR"
- **Tips**:
  - Validate willingness to pay
  - Test pricing models
  - Consider freemium

### 7. Cost Structure
What are your major costs?
- **Format**: List of cost categories
- **Example**: "Engineering salaries, AWS hosting, sales"
- **Tips**:
  - Focus on big-ticket items
  - Identify fixed vs variable
  - Plan for scale

### 8. Key Metrics
What are your most important numbers?
- **Format**: List of 3-5 key metrics
- **Example**: "DAU, conversion rate, NPS, MRR"
- **Tips**:
  - Focus on actionable metrics
  - Avoid vanity metrics
  - Set targets

### 9. Unfair Advantage
What can't be easily copied?
- **Format**: Description of competitive moat
- **Example**: "Proprietary data, network effects, exclusive partnerships"
- **Tips**:
  - Be honest (most startups have none)
  - Build over time
  - Hard to fake

## Problem-Solution Fit vs Product-Market Fit

### Problem-Solution Fit
- You understand the problem
- Your solution addresses it
- Customers agree you solve the problem
- **Test**: Willingness to use, qualitative feedback

### Product-Market Fit
- Market wants your product
- You can scale to meet demand
- Strong retention and growth
- **Test**: Sean Ellis (40%+ very disappointed), organic growth

## Building Your Lean Canvas

### Step 1: Start with Problem
- Talk to 10+ potential customers
- Identify common pain points
- Rank by severity

### Step 2: Define Customer Segments
- Who has these problems?
- What are their characteristics?
- Who is most accessible?

### Step 3: Craft UVP
- Combine problem + solution + benefit
- Make it memorable
- Test with customers

### Step 4: Outline Solution
- What features address the problems?
- What's the MVP?
- What can wait?

### Step 5: Identify Channels
- Where do customers spend time?
- How do they find solutions?
- What works for testing?

### Step 6: Model Revenue
- What's the pricing model?
- What are customers willing to pay?
- How does revenue scale?

### Step 7: Estimate Costs
- What do you need to build/operate?
- Who do you need to hire?
- What infrastructure?

### Step 8: Pick Key Metrics
- What indicates progress?
- What predicts success?
- What can you measure?

### Step 9: Identify Advantage
- What's hard to copy?
- What do you have that others don't?
- What can you build over time?

## Lean Canvas vs Business Model Canvas

| Aspect | Lean Canvas | Business Model Canvas |
|--------|-------------|----------------------|
| Focus | Startups, problem validation | Existing businesses |
| Problem emphasis | Strong | Weak |
| Solution emphasis | Strong | Weak |
| Metrics | Specific to startup | Generic |
| Competition | In "Problem" block | Separate block |
| Use case | Early stage | Established business |

## Common Lean Canvas Mistakes
1. Vague problem statements
2. Solution without validated problem
3. Targeting "everyone"
4. Weak or no unfair advantage
5. Ignoring unit economics
6. Too many features in MVP
7. Not testing with real customers
