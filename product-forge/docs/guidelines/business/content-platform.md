# Content Platform Business Model

## Overview
Content management, publishing, and consumption platforms.

## Core Processes
- Content creation and editing
- Content publishing and scheduling
- Content categorization and tagging
- User engagement and comments
- Content recommendations
- Monetization (ads, subscriptions)

## Common Integrations
- AWS S3 (media storage)
- CloudFront/Cloudflare (CDN)
- Algolia (search)
- Disqus (comments)
- Google AdSense (monetization)

## Key Metrics (KPIs)
- Page Views
- Time on Site
- Bounce Rate
- Content Shares
- Subscriber Growth
- Ad Revenue per Mille (RPM)

## Compliance Requirements
- DMCA (copyright)
- GDPR (if EU users)
- COPPA (if children's content)

## Recommended Tech Stack
- Frontend: React/Next.js
- Backend: Node.js/Python
- Database: PostgreSQL/MongoDB
- Hosting: AWS/GCP
- CDN: CloudFront/Cloudflare

## Common Pitfalls
1. Slow page load times
2. Poor content discovery
3. Weak moderation tools
4. Inadequate monetization
5. Mobile experience neglected
