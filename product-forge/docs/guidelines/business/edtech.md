# Education Technology (Edtech)

## Overview
Educational technology and e-learning

## Core Processes
- Course creation and management
- Student enrollment and onboarding
- Content delivery and streaming
- Assessment and grading
- Progress tracking and analytics
- Certificate generation

## Common Integrations
- Zoom (live classes)
- Vimeo/Wistia (video hosting)
- Stripe (payments)
- Google Classroom (LMS)
- H5P (interactive content)

## Key Metrics (KPIs)
- Course Completion Rate
- Student Engagement Score
- Learning Outcomes
- Revenue per Course
- Student Retention
- Net Promoter Score

## Compliance Requirements
- FERPA (US student data)
- COPPA (if children)
- GDPR (if EU students)
- Accessibility (WCAG)

## Recommended Tech Stack
- **frontend**: React/Next.js
- **backend**: Node.js/Python
- **database**: PostgreSQL/MongoDB
- **hosting**: AWS/GCP
- **video**: WebRTC/RTMP

## Common Pitfalls
1. Building features nobody uses
2. Ignoring user feedback
3. Underestimating time to market
4. Neglecting compliance requirements
5. Poor performance optimization
