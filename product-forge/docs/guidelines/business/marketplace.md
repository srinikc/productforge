# Marketplace Business Model

## Overview
Multi-sided platform connecting buyers and sellers.

## Core Processes
- Seller onboarding and verification
- Product/service listing management
- Search and discovery
- Order matching and fulfillment
- Payment processing and escrow
- Dispute resolution
- Reviews and ratings

## Common Integrations
- Stripe Connect (payments)
- Algolia (search)
- Twilio (notifications)
- Stripe Radar (fraud)
- Google Maps (location)

## Key Metrics (KPIs)
- Gross Merchandise Value (GMV)
- Take Rate
- Liquidity
- Time to First Transaction
- Seller Retention
- Buyer Retention

## Compliance Requirements
- GDPR (if EU users)
- Marketplace regulations
- Tax compliance (varies by region)

## Recommended Tech Stack
- Frontend: React/Next.js
- Backend: Node.js/Python
- Database: PostgreSQL
- Hosting: AWS/GCP
- Search: Elasticsearch/Algolia

## Common Pitfalls
1. Chicken-and-egg problem (no sellers without buyers)
2. Poor trust and safety measures
3. Complex payment flows
4. Inadequate dispute resolution
5. Weak seller tools
