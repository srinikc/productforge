# Compliance, Legal & Risk

## Overview
Compliance, legal, and risk management are critical for business sustainability. Non-compliance can result in fines, lawsuits, and business closure.

## Common Compliance Requirements

### 1. Data Privacy

#### GDPR (General Data Protection Regulation)
- **Region**: European Union
- **Applies to**: Any business processing EU resident data
- **Requirements**:
  - Lawful basis for processing
  - Explicit consent
  - Right to access data
  - Right to deletion
  - Data portability
  - 72-hour breach notification
  - DPO (Data Protection Officer) for large companies
- **Penalties**: Up to 4% global revenue or €20M

#### CCPA (California Consumer Privacy Act)
- **Region**: California, USA
- **Applies to**: Businesses with CA consumers meeting thresholds
- **Requirements**:
  - Right to know
  - Right to delete
  - Right to opt-out of sale
  - Non-discrimination
- **Penalties**: $2,500-$7,500 per violation

#### HIPAA (Health Insurance Portability and Accountability Act)
- **Region**: USA
- **Applies to**: Healthcare providers, insurers, business associates
- **Requirements**:
  - PHI protection
  - Security safeguards
  - Breach notification
  - Business Associate Agreements
- **Penalties**: $100-$50,000 per violation

#### COPPA (Children's Online Privacy Protection Act)
- **Region**: USA
- **Applies to**: Services for children under 13
- **Requirements**:
  - Parental consent
  - Privacy policy
  - Data minimization
- **Penalties**: Up to $42,530 per violation

#### PIPEDA (Personal Information Protection and Electronic Documents Act)
- **Region**: Canada
- **Applies to**: Commercial activities in Canada
- **Requirements**: Similar to GDPR

#### LGPD (Lei Geral de Proteção de Dados)
- **Region**: Brazil
- **Applies to**: Processing of Brazilian resident data
- **Requirements**: Similar to GDPR

### 2. Financial Compliance

#### PCI DSS (Payment Card Industry Data Security Standard)
- **Applies to**: Any business processing credit cards
- **Requirements**:
  - Secure network
  - Cardholder data protection
  - Access control
  - Regular testing
  - Security policies
- **Penalties**: Fines, loss of card processing

#### SOX (Sarbanes-Oxley Act)
- **Applies to**: Public companies in USA
- **Requirements**:
  - Internal controls
  - Financial reporting
  - CEO/CFO certification
  - Auditing requirements

#### AML (Anti-Money Laundering)
- **Applies to**: Financial institutions
- **Requirements**:
  - KYC (Know Your Customer)
  - Transaction monitoring
  - Suspicious activity reporting

#### KYC (Know Your Customer)
- **Applies to**: Financial services
- **Requirements**:
  - Identity verification
  - Risk assessment
  - Ongoing monitoring

### 3. Security Standards

#### SOC 2 (Service Organization Control 2)
- **Type**: Security audit
- **Categories**: Security, Availability, Processing Integrity, Confidentiality, Privacy
- **Use cases**: Required by many enterprise customers
- **Process**: Audit by CPA firm

#### ISO 27001
- **Type**: Information security management
- **Scope**: Entire organization
- **Use cases**: International security standard
- **Process**: Certification audit

#### NIST Cybersecurity Framework
- **Type**: Security framework
- **Functions**: Identify, Protect, Detect, Respond, Recover
- **Use cases**: US government and contractors

### 4. Industry-Specific

#### FERPA (Family Educational Rights and Privacy Act)
- **Applies to**: Educational institutions
- **Requirements**: Student data protection

#### GLBA (Gramm-Leach-Bliley Act)
- **Applies to**: Financial institutions
- **Requirements**: Consumer financial information protection

#### FINRA (Financial Industry Regulatory Authority)
- **Applies to**: Brokerage firms
- **Requirements**: Securities trading compliance

#### FDA (Food and Drug Administration)
- **Applies to**: Medical devices, drugs, food
- **Requirements**: Approval process, labeling, safety monitoring

#### FTC (Federal Trade Commission)
- **Applies to**: Consumer protection
- **Requirements**: Truth in advertising, anti-competitive practices

## Legal Documents

### 1. Terms of Service
- **Purpose**: Governs use of product
- **Includes**: User obligations, disclaimers, liability limits
- **When needed**: Before accepting users
- **Reviewed by**: Lawyer

### 2. Privacy Policy
- **Purpose**: Discloses data practices
- **Includes**: Data collected, usage, sharing, security
- **When needed**: Before collecting data
- **Reviewed by**: Lawyer + privacy specialist

### 3. Cookie Policy
- **Purpose**: Discloses cookie usage
- **When needed**: Before using cookies
- **Includes**: Types of cookies, purposes, opt-out

### 4. Data Processing Agreement (DPA)
- **Purpose**: GDPR compliance
- **Parties**: Controller and processor
- **Required for**: EU data processing

### 5. Service Level Agreement (SLA)
- **Purpose**: Defines service guarantees
- **Includes**: Uptime, support, penalties
- **When needed**: B2B contracts

### 6. Master Service Agreement (MSA)
- **Purpose**: Overall contract terms
- **Includes**: Payment, IP, liability, termination
- **When needed**: B2B contracts

### 7. Non-Disclosure Agreement (NDA)
- **Purpose**: Protect confidential information
- **When needed**: Before sharing confidential info
- **Types**: Mutual, one-way

### 8. End User License Agreement (EULA)
- **Purpose**: Software licensing terms
- **When needed**: Software products
- **Includes**: License scope, restrictions, termination

## Risk Management

### 1. Risk Categories

#### Strategic Risk
- Competition
- Market changes
- Technology disruption
- Business model viability

#### Operational Risk
- System failures
- Process breakdowns
- Human error
- Supply chain issues

#### Financial Risk
- Cash flow problems
- Currency fluctuations
- Credit risk
- Market volatility

#### Compliance Risk
- Regulatory changes
- Non-compliance penalties
- Audit findings
- License requirements

#### Reputational Risk
- Public relations crises
- Customer dissatisfaction
- Data breaches
- Social media backlash

#### Security Risk
- Cyber attacks
- Data breaches
- Insider threats
- Third-party vulnerabilities

### 2. Risk Assessment

#### Risk Register
- **Risk ID**
- **Description**
- **Likelihood** (1-5)
- **Impact** (1-5)
- **Risk Score** (Likelihood × Impact)
- **Mitigation Plan**
- **Owner**

#### Risk Response Strategies
1. **Avoid**: Eliminate the risk
2. **Mitigate**: Reduce likelihood or impact
3. **Transfer**: Insurance, contracts
4. **Accept**: Acknowledge and monitor

### 3. Insurance

#### Common Business Insurance
- **General Liability**: Lawsuits, injuries
- **Professional Liability**: Errors and omissions
- **Cyber Insurance**: Data breaches
- **Product Liability**: Product defects
- **Workers' Compensation**: Employee injuries
- **Key Person Insurance**: Loss of key employees
- **Business Interruption**: Lost income

1. Not understanding applicable regulations
2. Treating compliance as checkbox
3. Inadequate documentation
4. Not training employees
5. Ignoring third-party risks
6. Not having incident response plan
7. Delaying compliance investments
