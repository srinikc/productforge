# Customer Acquisition

## Overview
Customer acquisition is the process of bringing new customers to your business. It's the top of the funnel and critical for growth.

## Acquisition Channels

### 1. Organic / Inbound

#### Content Marketing
- Blog posts, articles, guides
- SEO optimization
- Thought leadership
- Best for: Building trust, long-term traffic

#### SEO (Search Engine Optimization)
- Keyword research
- On-page optimization
- Link building
- Technical SEO
- Best for: Sustainable, long-term traffic

#### Social Media
- Organic social posts
- Community building
- Platform-specific strategies
- Best for: Brand awareness, engagement

#### Referral Programs
- Existing customers refer new ones
- Incentives for both parties
- Word-of-mouth amplification
- Best for: High-trust products

#### Virality
- Built-in sharing mechanics
- Network effects
- Word-of-mouth growth
- Best for: Consumer apps, social products

### 2. Paid

#### Search Ads (SEM/PPC)
- Google Ads, Bing Ads
- Keyword bidding
- Landing page optimization
- Best for: High-intent traffic

#### Social Ads
- Facebook/Instagram Ads
- LinkedIn Ads (B2B)
- Twitter/X Ads
- TikTok Ads
- Best for: Targeted reach

#### Display & Retargeting
- Banner ads
- Retargeting visitors
- Programmatic advertising
- Best for: Brand awareness, remarketing

#### Influencer Marketing
- Partner with influencers
- Sponsored content
- Affiliate programs
- Best for: Consumer products, lifestyle

### 3. Outbound

#### Cold Email
- Personalized outreach
- A/B testing subject lines
- Follow-up sequences
- Best for: B2B sales

#### Cold Calling
- Phone-based outreach
- Qualifying leads
- Setting meetings
- Best for: Enterprise B2B

#### LinkedIn Outreach
- Connection requests
- Direct messages
- Content engagement
- Best for: B2B sales

#### Direct Mail
- Physical mailers
- Personalized packages
- Events and conferences
- Best for: High-value B2B

### 4. Partnerships

#### Channel Partners
- Resellers, integrators
- Revenue sharing
- Co-marketing
- Best for: Enterprise software

#### Integration Partners
- Product integrations
- Co-marketing
- Shared customers
- Best for: SaaS, platforms

#### Affiliate Programs
- Third-party promoters
- Commission-based
- Performance marketing
- Best for: E-commerce, online services

## Customer Acquisition Metrics

### Key Metrics
- **CAC (Customer Acquisition Cost)**: Total acquisition spend / new customers
- **CAC Payback Period**: Months to recover CAC
- **LTV/CAC Ratio**: Should be 3:1 or higher
- **Conversion Rate**: % of visitors who become customers
- **Click-Through Rate (CTR)**: % who click on ad/email
- **Cost Per Click (CPC)**: Cost per ad click
- **Cost Per Lead (CPL)**: Cost per qualified lead
- **Cost Per Acquisition (CPA)**: Cost per customer
- **Return on Ad Spend (ROAS)**: Revenue / ad spend
- **Organic Traffic %**: % of traffic from organic sources

## Acquisition Funnel Stages
1. **Awareness**: User learns about product
2. **Interest**: User shows interest
3. **Consideration**: User evaluates product
4. **Intent**: User signals buying intent
5. **Evaluation**: User compares options
6. **Purchase**: User becomes customer

## Channel Selection Framework
Choose channels based on:
1. **Target audience**: Where do they spend time?
2. **Product type**: B2B vs B2C, high-ticket vs low-ticket
3. **Budget**: Paid vs organic
4. **Speed**: How fast do you need results?
5. **Scalability**: Can you scale this channel?
6. **Measurability**: Can you track ROI?

## Common Mistakes
1. Spreading budget too thin across channels
2. Not measuring channel ROI
3. Optimizing for vanity metrics
4. Ignoring organic channels
5. Not testing and iterating
6. Poor landing page conversion
7. No clear value proposition
