# Go-to-Market (GTM) Strategies

## Overview
A go-to-market strategy is how a company brings a product to market and reaches its target customers. It's the bridge between product development and sales.

## Key Components of a GTM Strategy

### 1. Target Audience
- Who is your ideal customer?
- What problems do they have?
- Where do they spend time?
- How do they make buying decisions?

### 2. Value Proposition
- What unique value do you provide?
- How is it different from competitors?
- Why should customers choose you?

### 3. Pricing & Packaging
- How will you price the product?
- What tiers/packages will you offer?
- How does pricing align with value?

### 4. Distribution Channels
- How will customers find you?
- Direct sales, self-serve, partner channel?
- Online, offline, hybrid?

### 5. Marketing & Positioning
- What is your brand positioning?
- What channels will you use?
- What is your messaging?

### 6. Sales Process
- How will you convert leads?
- Inside sales, field sales, PLG?
- Sales cycle length?

## Common GTM Strategies

### 1. Product-Led Growth (PLG)
- Product is the primary driver of acquisition
- Free tier or trial, self-serve signup
- Viral loops within product
- Best for: SaaS, consumer apps
- Examples: Slack, Dropbox, Notion

### 2. Sales-Led Growth (SLG)
- Sales team is primary driver
- Outbound prospecting, demos, negotiations
- Longer sales cycle, higher ACV
- Best for: Enterprise B2B
- Examples: Salesforce, Oracle, SAP

### 3. Marketing-Led Growth (MLG)
- Marketing team is primary driver
- Content marketing, SEO, paid ads
- Lead generation through content
- Best for: B2B with long sales cycles
- Examples: HubSpot, Mailchimp

### 4. Community-Led Growth (CLG)
- Community is primary driver
- Build engaged community first
- Community members become customers
- Best for: Developer tools, niche products
- Examples: Figma, Discord, GitHub

### 5. Partner-Led Growth
- Partners are primary distribution
- Resellers, integrators, agencies
- Channel partnerships
- Best for: Complex products needing services
- Examples: AWS, Microsoft

### 6. Hybrid GTM
- Combination of multiple strategies
- PLG for SMB, SLG for enterprise
- Different strategies for different segments
- Best for: Companies with multiple segments

## GTM Planning Process

1. **Market Research**
   - Market size (TAM, SAM, SOM)
   - Competitive landscape
   - Customer needs and pain points

2. **Customer Segmentation**
   - Identify target segments
   - Build personas
   - Map customer journey

3. **Positioning & Messaging**
   - Unique value proposition
   - Key messages
   - Brand voice

4. **Channel Strategy**
   - Choose distribution channels
   - Partner strategy
   - Online vs offline

5. **Pricing & Packaging**
   - Set prices
   - Create tiers
   - Define discount strategy

6. **Launch Plan**
   - Pre-launch activities
   - Launch day activities
   - Post-launch follow-up

7. **Metrics & KPIs**
   - Customer Acquisition Cost (CAC)
   - Lifetime Value (LTV)
   - Payback Period
   - Conversion rates

## GTM Mistakes to Avoid
1. Not defining target customer clearly
2. Trying to serve everyone
3. Ignoring competitive landscape
4. Underestimating sales cycle
5. Not aligning sales and marketing
6. Launching without validation
7. Ignoring customer feedback post-launch
