# Growth Patterns & Loops

## Overview
Growth patterns and loops are systematic, repeatable mechanisms that drive sustainable business growth. They are the engines of viral, compound growth.

## Core Growth Loops

### 1. Viral Loop
- **How it works**: User invites others → Others join → They invite more
- **Key metric**: Viral coefficient (K-factor)
- **Best for**: Consumer apps, social products
- **Examples**: WhatsApp, Zoom, TikTok
- **Formula**: K = (invites per user) × (conversion rate of invite)

### 2. Content Loop
- **How it works**: Create content → SEO/discovery → More visitors → More content
- **Key metric**: Organic traffic growth, content velocity
- **Best for**: Content platforms, SaaS blogs
- **Examples**: HubSpot, Ahrefs

### 3. Paid Loop
- **How it works**: Revenue → Reinvest in ads → More customers → More revenue
- **Key metric**: LTV/CAC ratio, ROAS
- **Best for**: Any business with positive unit economics
- **Examples**: Most e-commerce, D2C brands

### 4. Sales Loop
- **How it works**: Sales reps → Customers → Revenue → More reps → More customers
- **Key metric**: Sales productivity, ramp time
- **Best for**: Enterprise B2B
- **Examples**: Salesforce, Gong

### 5. UGC Loop (User-Generated Content)
- **How it works**: Users create content → More content attracts users → More users create content
- **Key metric**: Content per user, engagement rate
- **Best for**: Social platforms, marketplaces
- **Examples**: Instagram, YouTube, Airbnb

### 6. Network Effect Loop
- **How it works**: More users → More value → More users
- **Key metric**: Network density, value per user
- **Best for**: Marketplaces, platforms
- **Examples**: Uber, Airbnb, LinkedIn

### 7. Data Network Effect
- **How it works**: More users → More data → Better product → More users
- **Key metric**: Data volume, model accuracy
- **Best for**: AI/ML products
- **Examples**: Google Search, Waze

### 8. Habit Loop
- **How it works**: Trigger → Action → Reward → Repeat
- **Key metric**: DAU/MAU, session frequency
- **Best for**: Consumer apps
- **Examples**: Instagram, TikTok, Slack

### 9. Marketplace Liquidity Loop
- **How it works**: More sellers → More buyers → More sellers
- **Key metric**: Time to first transaction, fill rate
- **Best for**: Marketplaces
- **Examples**: eBay, Uber, DoorDash

### 10. API/Integration Loop
- **How it works**: More integrations → More value → More users → More integrations
- **Key metric**: Integration count, ecosystem size
- **Best for**: Platforms, developer tools
- **Examples**: Slack, Zapier, Twilio

## Growth Stages

### Stage 1: PMF (Product-Market Fit)
- **Focus**: Find product-market fit
- **Key metric**: Sean Ellis test (40%+ would be "very disappointed")
- **Activities**: User research, iteration, retention
- **Duration**: 6-18 months

### Stage 2: Early Growth
- **Focus**: Find scalable acquisition channel
- **Key metric**: CAC payback, LTV/CAC
- **Activities**: Channel testing, optimization
- **Duration**: 6-12 months

### Stage 3: Scale
- **Focus**: Scale what works
- **Key metric**: Revenue growth rate, efficiency
- **Activities**: Team building, process optimization
- **Duration**: 1-3 years

### Stage 4: Maturity
- **Focus**: Defend market position
- **Key metric**: Market share, NRR
- **Activities**: Expansion, new products, M&A
- **Duration**: Ongoing

## Growth Frameworks

### AARRR (Pirate Metrics)
- **Acquisition**: How do users find you?
- **Activation**: Do they have a great first experience?
- **Retention**: Do they come back?
- **Referral**: Do they tell others?
- **Revenue**: Do they pay?

### AAARRR (with Awareness)
- **Awareness**: Do they know you exist?
- Plus the AARRR stages

### RICE Scoring (for experiments)
- **Reach**: How many users affected?
- **Impact**: How much impact per user?
- **Confidence**: How confident in the impact?
- **Effort**: How much effort required?

### ICE Scoring
- **Impact**: Potential impact
- **Confidence**: Confidence in success
- **Ease**: Ease of implementation

## North Star Metric

A single metric that best captures the value your product delivers to customers.

### Good North Star Examples
- Airbnb: Nights booked
- Spotify: Time spent listening
- Facebook: Daily active users
- Amazon: Purchases per month
- Uber: Rides per week

### Choosing Your North Star
1. Reflects customer value
2. Leading indicator of revenue
3. Actionable by team
4. Measurable accurately

## Growth Team Structure

### Models
1. **Growth Team**: Dedicated team owns growth
2. **Growth Pods**: Cross-functional pods
3. **Growth Sprinkles**: Everyone owns growth
4. **Functional Growth**: Growth within each function

### Team Composition
- Product Manager
- Engineers (2-4)
- Designer
- Data Analyst
- Marketing (sometimes)

## Growth Experimentation

### Process
1. **Hypothesis**: What do you believe?
2. **Experiment**: How will you test?
3. **Measure**: What are the results?
4. **Learn**: What did you learn?
5. **Iterate**: What next?

### Best Practices
- Run experiments in parallel
- Statistical significance
- Document learnings
- Share across team
- Kill bad ideas fast
- Double down on winners

## Common Growth Mistakes
1. Growing before PMF
2. Focusing on vanity metrics
3. Ignoring retention
4. Not measuring unit economics
5. Spreading too thin
6. No experimentation process
7. Not investing in data
