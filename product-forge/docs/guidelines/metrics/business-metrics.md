# Key Business Metrics

## Overview
Metrics are the compass for your business. The right metrics at the right time help you make better decisions and identify issues early.

## Metric Categories

### 1. Growth Metrics

#### Revenue Growth
- **MRR Growth Rate**: (Current MRR - Previous MRR) / Previous MRR
- **ARR Growth Rate**: Same for annual
- **YoY Growth**: Year over year comparison
- **Target**: 10-20% MoM early, 5-10% later

#### User Growth
- **New Users**: Net new users in period
- **Total Users**: Cumulative users
- **Growth Rate**: User growth %
- **Target**: 10-30% MoM early stage

#### Market Share
- **Definition**: Your revenue / Total market revenue
- **Use cases**: Competitive positioning
- **Target**: Dominant in niche, growing in larger markets

### 2. Engagement Metrics

#### DAU/MAU Ratio
- **Definition**: Daily active / Monthly active
- **Target**: >20% means daily habit
- **Use cases**: Stickiness, retention

#### Session Metrics
- **Session Length**: Time per session
- **Sessions per User**: Frequency
- **Pages per Session**: Depth
- **Target**: Varies by product

#### Feature Usage
- **% of users using feature X**
- **Frequency of feature use**
- **Depth of feature engagement**
- **Use cases**: Product roadmap, retention

### 3. Retention Metrics

#### Cohort Retention
- **Definition**: % of users from a cohort still active
- **Time periods**: D1, D7, D30, W1, M1, M3, M6
- **Targets**:
  - D1: >40%
  - D7: >20%
  - D30: >10%

#### Churn Rate
- **Customer Churn**: % of customers lost
- **Revenue Churn**: % of revenue lost
- **Logo Churn vs Revenue Churn**: Different metrics
- **Target**: <5% monthly for SaaS

#### Net Revenue Retention
- **Definition**: (Starting + Expansion - Churn) / Starting
- **Target**: >100% is excellent
- **Use cases**: Expansion vs contraction

### 4. Financial Metrics

#### Revenue
- **MRR**: Monthly recurring revenue
- **ARR**: Annual recurring revenue
- **Total Revenue**: All revenue sources
- **Revenue per Employee**: Efficiency

#### Profitability
- **Gross Margin**: (Revenue - COGS) / Revenue
- **Operating Margin**: Operating Income / Revenue
- **Net Margin**: Net Income / Revenue
- **EBITDA**: Earnings before interest, tax, D&A

#### Cash
- **Cash on Hand**: Total cash reserves
- **Burn Rate**: Monthly cash spending
- **Runway**: Months of cash remaining
- **Target**: 12-18 months runway

### 5. Sales Metrics

#### Pipeline Metrics
- **Pipeline Value**: Total opportunities
- **Pipeline Coverage**: Pipeline / Quota (target 3-4x)
- **Win Rate**: % of deals won
- **Average Deal Size**: Average revenue per deal

#### Sales Activity
- **Calls per Rep**: Daily call volume
- **Emails per Rep**: Outreach volume
- **Demos per Rep**: Demo volume
- **Conversion by Stage**: Funnel efficiency

#### Sales Cycle
- **Length**: Days from lead to close
- **By Segment**: Different cycles for different segments
- **Trends**: Shortening over time = efficiency

### 6. Marketing Metrics

#### Awareness
- **Brand Mentions**: Volume of brand mentions
- **Share of Voice**: Your brand vs competitors
- **Direct Traffic**: Visits typing URL directly
- **Search Volume**: Brand search volume

#### Acquisition
- **Traffic by Source**: Channel breakdown
- **Conversion Rate**: Visitors to leads
- **CPL**: Cost per lead
- **MQLs/SQLs**: Lead quality

#### Content
- **Page Views**: Total content views
- **Time on Page**: Engagement
- **Social Shares**: Content virality
- **Backlinks**: SEO value

### 7. Customer Success Metrics

#### Health Score
- **Product Usage**: How often they use
- **Support Tickets**: Volume and severity
- **Engagement**: Email opens, event attendance
- **Payment History**: On-time payments
- **NPS**: Net Promoter Score

#### NPS (Net Promoter Score)
- **Definition**: % Promoters - % Detractors
- **Scale**: -100 to +100
- **Target**: >30 is good, >50 is excellent
- **Use cases**: Predictor of growth

#### CSAT (Customer Satisfaction)
- **Definition**: % satisfied with interaction
- **Scale**: 1-5 typically
- **Target**: >80% satisfied

### 8. Product Metrics

#### Activation Rate
- **Definition**: % of signups who reach activation event
- **Activation Event**: The "aha moment"
- **Target**: >40% for consumer, >60% for B2B

#### Time to Value
- **Definition**: Time from signup to first value
- **Target**: <5 minutes consumer, <1 day B2B
- **Use cases**: Onboarding optimization

#### Feature Adoption
- **% of users using new features**
- **Time to adoption**: How long to reach 50% adoption
- **Use cases**: Launch success

## Metrics by Stage

### Pre-Product/Market Fit
- User research depth
- Problem validation
- Willingness to pay
- Qualitative feedback
- Retention curves

### Early Stage
- Activation rate
- Retention curves
- Qualitative feedback
- Time to value
- NPS

### Growth Stage
- CAC and LTV
- Payback period
- Channel efficiency
- NRR
- Magic number

### Scale Stage
- Market share
- Revenue growth
- Profitability
- Cash efficiency
- NRR and gross retention

## Common Metrics Mistakes
1. Vanity metrics (total users vs active users)
2. Wrong metrics for stage
3. Too many metrics (focus on 3-5)
4. Not segmenting metrics
5. Ignoring leading indicators
6. Not acting on metrics
7. Measuring without context

## Metric Frameworks

### AARRR (Pirate Metrics)
- Acquisition, Activation, Retention, Referral, Revenue

### HEART (Google)
- Happiness, Engagement, Adoption, Retention, Task success

### North Star Metric
- Single metric that captures value delivery
