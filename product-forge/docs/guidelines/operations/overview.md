# Business Operations

## Overview
Business operations encompasses the day-to-day activities and processes that keep a business running. Efficient operations are critical for scaling.

## Core Operational Functions

### 1. Sales Operations

#### Sales Process
1. **Lead Generation**: Find potential customers
2. **Lead Qualification**: Determine fit and intent
3. **Discovery**: Understand needs
4. **Demo/Presentation**: Show solution
5. **Proposal**: Formal offer
6. **Negotiation**: Address concerns
7. **Closing**: Get commitment
8. **Onboarding**: Transition to customer success

#### Sales Tech Stack
- **CRM**: Salesforce, HubSpot, Pipedrive
- **Sales Engagement**: Outreach, Salesloft
- **Calling**: Aircall, Dialpad
- **Scheduling**: Calendly, Chili Piper
- **Proposals**: PandaDoc, DocSend
- **Forecasting**: Clari, Gong

#### Sales Metrics
- Pipeline coverage (3-4x)
- Win rate (20-30% typical)
- Average deal size
- Sales cycle length
- Ramp time (time to productivity)
- Quota attainment

#### Sales Playbook
- Ideal customer profile
- Buyer personas
- Discovery questions
- Objection handling
- Competitive positioning
- Case studies

### 2. Marketing Operations

#### Marketing Channels
- **Content**: Blog, video, podcast
- **SEO**: Organic search
- **Paid**: Search, social, display
- **Email**: Nurture campaigns
- **Social**: Organic and paid
- **Events**: Webinars, conferences
- **PR**: Media coverage, analyst relations
- **Partner**: Co-marketing

#### Marketing Funnel
- **TOFU (Top of Funnel)**: Awareness
- **MOFU (Middle of Funnel)**: Consideration
- **BOFU (Bottom of Funnel)**: Decision
- **Content mapping**: Content to funnel stage

#### Marketing Tech Stack
- **Analytics**: Google Analytics, Mixpanel
- **Email**: Mailchimp, Customer.io
- **Automation**: HubSpot, Marketo
- **Social**: Buffer, Hootsuite
- **SEO**: Ahrefs, SEMrush
- **Attribution**: Bizible, Attribution

#### Marketing Metrics
- CAC by channel
- Conversion rates by funnel stage
- Content engagement
- Email open/click rates
- Social engagement
- MQL to SQL conversion

### 3. Customer Support Operations

#### Support Channels
- **Email**: Traditional support
- **Chat**: Live chat
- **Phone**: Voice support
- **Self-Service**: Knowledge base, FAQs
- **Community**: User forums
- **Social**: Twitter, Facebook
- **In-App**: Within product

#### Support Tiers
- **Tier 1**: Basic questions, common issues
- **Tier 2**: Technical issues, bug investigation
- **Tier 3**: Complex issues, engineering involvement
- **Tier 4**: Critical incidents, executive escalation

#### Support Metrics
- First Response Time (FRT)
- Resolution Time
- First Contact Resolution (FCR)
- Customer Satisfaction (CSAT)
- Net Promoter Score (NPS)
- Ticket Volume
- Cost per Ticket

#### Support Tech Stack
- **Helpdesk**: Zendesk, Intercom, Freshdesk
- **Knowledge Base**: Document360, HelpScout
- **Live Chat**: Intercom, Drift
- **Phone**: Aircall, Talkdesk
- **Community**: Discourse, Circle

### 4. Finance Operations

#### Key Financial Processes
- **Revenue Recognition**: When to count revenue
- **Invoicing**: Bill customers
- **Collections**: Get paid
- **Expense Management**: Track spending
- **Budgeting**: Plan finances
- **Forecasting**: Project future
- **Reporting**: Financial statements
- **Tax Compliance**: File taxes
- **Audit**: External review

#### Financial Metrics
- **Gross Margin**: Revenue - COGS
- **Operating Margin**: Operating Income / Revenue
- **Burn Rate**: Monthly cash spending
- **Runway**: Months of cash remaining
- **ARR/MRR**: Annual/Monthly Recurring Revenue
- **Cash Conversion Cycle**: Days to convert to cash

#### Finance Tech Stack
- **Accounting**: QuickBooks, Xero, NetSuite
- **Billing**: Stripe, Chargebee
- **Expense**: Expensify, Brex
- **Forecasting**: Mosaic, Causal
- **Reporting**: Fathom, Spotlight

### 5. People Operations (HR)

#### Core HR Processes
- **Recruiting**: Find and hire talent
- **Onboarding**: Integrate new hires
- **Performance Management**: Reviews and feedback
- **Compensation**: Salaries and equity
- **Benefits**: Health, retirement, etc.
- **Training**: Skill development
- **Culture**: Values and engagement
- **Offboarding**: Exit process

#### HR Metrics
- Time to Hire
- Cost per Hire
- Employee Turnover
- Engagement Score
- Performance Distribution
- Diversity Metrics

#### HR Tech Stack
- **HRIS**: Rippling, Gusto, Workday
- **Recruiting**: Greenhouse, Lever
- **Performance**: Lattice, 15Five
- **Payroll**: Gusto, Rippling
- **Benefits**: Justworks, Sequoia

### 6. Product Operations

#### Product Development Process
1. **Discovery**: Research, user interviews
2. **Strategy**: Roadmap, prioritization
3. **Design**: Wireframes, prototypes
4. **Development**: Build features
5. **Testing**: QA, user testing
6. **Launch**: Release to users
7. **Measure**: Track metrics
8. **Iterate**: Improve based on data

#### Product Frameworks
- **RICE**: Reach, Impact, Confidence, Effort
- **ICE**: Impact, Confidence, Ease
- **MoSCoW**: Must, Should, Could, Won't
- **Kano**: Basic, Performance, Excitement

#### Product Metrics
- Activation rate
- Feature adoption
- Time to value
- User engagement
- Retention curves

## Operational Challenges by Stage

### Early Stage
- Founder does everything
- Hire generalists
- Build processes as you go
- Manual everything

### Growth Stage
- Hire specialists
- Document processes
- Implement systems
- Start delegation

### Scale Stage
- Optimize processes
- Automate everything
- Build middle management
- Measure and improve

## Common Operations Mistakes
1. Not documenting processes
2. Over-engineering early
3. Not measuring efficiency
4. Ignoring culture
5. Under-investing in tools
6. Poor vendor management
7. Not planning for scale
