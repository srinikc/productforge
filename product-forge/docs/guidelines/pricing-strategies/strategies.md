# Pricing Strategies

## Overview
Pricing is one of the most important levers for business success. The right pricing strategy can dramatically impact adoption, revenue, and profitability.

## Common Pricing Strategies

### 1. Cost-Plus Pricing
- **Description**: Add a markup to the cost of producing the product
- **Formula**: Price = Cost + Markup%
- **Pros**: Simple, ensures profitability
- **Cons**: Ignores customer value perception
- **Best for**: Manufacturing, commodities

### 2. Value-Based Pricing
- **Description**: Set price based on perceived customer value
- **Pros**: Maximizes revenue when value is high
- **Cons**: Difficult to measure value accurately
- **Best for**: SaaS, enterprise software, services

### 3. Competitive Pricing
- **Description**: Set prices based on competitors
- **Pros**: Market-aligned, reduces risk
- **Cons**: Race to bottom, ignores differentiation
- **Best for**: Commoditized markets

### 4. Penetration Pricing
- **Description**: Low initial price to gain market share
- **Pros**: Fast adoption, market share growth
- **Cons**: Low margins, hard to raise prices later
- **Best for**: New market entry, network effects

### 5. Price Skimming
- **Description**: High initial price, lower over time
- **Pros**: Captures early adopters, high margins
- **Cons**: Slow adoption, attracts competitors
- **Best for**: Innovative products, early adopters

### 6. Freemium
- **Description**: Free tier + paid premium features
- **Pros**: Low barrier to entry, viral potential
- **Cons**: Conversion rate typically <5%
- **Best for**: Products with viral potential

### 7. Tiered Pricing
- **Description**: Multiple price points for different features/usage
- **Pros**: Captures different customer segments
- **Cons**: Complex to design and maintain
- **Best for**: SaaS, platforms

### 8. Dynamic Pricing
- **Description**: Price changes based on demand/supply
- **Pros**: Maximizes revenue in variable markets
- **Cons**: Customer perception issues
- **Best for**: Travel, hospitality, utilities

### 9. Bundle Pricing
- **Description**: Multiple products sold together at discount
- **Pros**: Increases average order value
- **Cons**: Cannibalization risk
- **Best for**: Complementary products

### 10. Psychological Pricing
- **Description**: Prices designed to influence perception ($9.99 vs $10)
- **Pros**: Simple to implement
- **Cons**: Limited impact, can feel manipulative
- **Best for**: Consumer products

## Pricing for SaaS

### Common SaaS Pricing Models
1. **Per-seat**: Charge per user (Slack, GitHub)
2. **Per-usage**: Charge per API call, GB stored (AWS)
3. **Tiered**: Multiple plans with feature gates (HubSpot)
4. **Flat-rate**: Single price for all features (Basecamp)
5. **Freemium**: Free + paid (LinkedIn, Dropbox)

### Pricing Page Best Practices
- Show 3-4 tiers (too many = decision paralysis)
- Highlight recommended/popular plan
- Annual vs monthly toggle (annual = 20% discount typical)
- Clear feature comparison
- Money-back guarantee for new customers
- Enterprise/custom contact option

### Price Optimization
- Test pricing with A/B tests
- Survey customers on willingness to pay
- Monitor churn by price point
- Adjust annually based on value delivered

## Common Pricing Mistakes
1. Pricing too low (leaves money on table)
2. Pricing too high (low adoption)
3. No clear differentiation between tiers
4. Hidden costs that surprise customers
5. Not adjusting for inflation/value delivered
6. Copying competitors without thinking about your value
