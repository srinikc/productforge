# Financial Models

## Overview
Financial models help you understand, project, and communicate the financial performance of your business. They are essential for planning, fundraising, and decision-making.

## Key Financial Statements

### 1. Income Statement (P&L)
Shows revenue, expenses, and profit over time.

#### Structure
- **Revenue**: All income
- **COGS (Cost of Goods Sold)**: Direct costs
- **Gross Profit**: Revenue - COGS
- **Operating Expenses**: Sales, R&D, G&A
- **Operating Income**: Gross Profit - OpEx
- **Interest, Taxes**: Non-operating items
- **Net Income**: Bottom line profit/loss

#### Key Metrics
- **Gross Margin**: Gross Profit / Revenue
- **Operating Margin**: Operating Income / Revenue
- **Net Margin**: Net Income / Revenue

### 2. Balance Sheet
Shows assets, liabilities, and equity at a point in time.

#### Structure
- **Assets**: Cash, receivables, inventory, equipment
- **Liabilities**: Payables, debt, deferred revenue
- **Equity**: Investment, retained earnings

#### Key Equation
Assets = Liabilities + Equity

### 3. Cash Flow Statement
Shows cash inflows and outflows.

#### Sections
- **Operating Activities**: Core business
- **Investing Activities**: CapEx, acquisitions
- **Financing Activities**: Debt, equity, dividends

## SaaS Financial Model

### Key Components

#### Revenue
- **MRR**: Monthly Recurring Revenue
- **ARR**: Annual Recurring Revenue
- **New MRR**: From new customers
- **Expansion MRR**: From upsells/cross-sells
- **Churned MRR**: From cancellations
- **Net New MRR**: New + Expansion - Churn

#### Costs
- **COGS**: Hosting, support, payment processing
- **Sales & Marketing**: Sales team, marketing spend
- **R&D**: Engineering, product
- **G&A**: Admin, finance, legal

#### Key Metrics
- **Gross Margin**: 70-85% target
- **CAC**: Customer Acquisition Cost
- **LTV**: Customer Lifetime Value
- **LTV/CAC**: 3:1 target
- **CAC Payback**: <12 months target
- **Magic Number**: Sales efficiency
- **Rule of 40**: Growth + Margin > 40%

### SaaS Unit Economics

#### LTV Calculation
LTV = (ARPU × Gross Margin) / Monthly Churn Rate

#### CAC Calculation
CAC = Total S&M Spend / New Customers

#### LTV/CAC
- <1: Losing money
- 1-3: Sustainable
- 3-5: Healthy
- >5: Underinvesting

#### CAC Payback
Payback = CAC / (ARPU × Gross Margin)
- <12 months: Excellent
- 12-24 months: Good
- >24 months: Concerning

## E-commerce Financial Model

### Key Components

#### Revenue
- **Orders per month**
- **Average Order Value (AOV)**
- **Total Revenue = Orders × AOV**

#### Costs
- **COGS**: Product costs
- **Shipping**: Fulfillment
- **Payment Processing**: 2-3% of revenue
- **Returns**: 10-30% of revenue
- **Marketing**: Variable by channel
- **Overhead**: Fixed costs

#### Key Metrics
- **Gross Margin**: 40-60% target
- **Contribution Margin**: After variable costs
- **CAC**: By channel
- **LTV**: From repeat purchases
- **Payback Period**: Time to recover CAC

## Marketplace Financial Model

### Key Components

#### Revenue
- **GMV (Gross Merchandise Value)**: Total transaction value
- **Take Rate**: % of GMV you keep
- **Net Revenue = GMV × Take Rate**

#### Costs
- **COGS**: Payment processing, hosting
- **Acquisition**: Both sides of marketplace
- **Trust & Safety**: Verification, support
- **Operations**: Customer support

#### Key Metrics
- **Liquidity**: Time to match
- **Fill Rate**: % of requests filled
- **Take Rate**: 5-30% typical
- **CAC by Side**: Buyer vs seller
- **LTV by Side**: Different lifetimes

## Budgeting & Forecasting

### 1. Top-Down Budgeting
- Start with overall revenue target
- Allocate to departments
- Bottom-up from department needs
- Reconcile differences

### 2. Bottom-Up Budgeting
- Each department builds budget
- Aggregate to company
- Top-down review
- Final approval

### 3. Driver-Based Planning
- Identify key drivers (customers, usage, etc.)
- Model relationships
- Forecast based on drivers
- More accurate than linear

### 4. Rolling Forecast
- 12-18 month forward view
- Updated monthly
- Adapts to actual results
- More accurate than annual budget

## Pricing Models

### 1. Cost-Plus
- **Formula**: Price = Cost + Markup
- **Use**: Manufacturing, commodities

### 2. Value-Based
- **Formula**: Price = Perceived Value
- **Use**: SaaS, enterprise

### 3. Competitive
- **Formula**: Price = Competitor ± Adjustment
- **Use**: Commoditized markets

### 4. Dynamic
- **Formula**: Price = f(Demand, Supply, Other)
- **Use**: Travel, hospitality

## Fundraising Models

### 1. Pre-Seed
- **Amount**: $50K-$500K
- **Investors**: Friends, family, angels
- **Valuation**: $1M-$5M
- **Use**: Product development

### 2. Seed
- **Amount**: $500K-$5M
- **Investors**: Angels, seed VCs
- **Valuation**: $5M-$30M
- **Use**: Initial traction

### 3. Series A
- **Amount**: $2M-$15M
- **Investors**: VCs
- **Valuation**: $15M-$50M
- **Use**: Scale to product-market fit

### 4. Series B
- **Amount**: $10M-$50M
- **Investors**: VCs
- **Valuation**: $50M-$200M
- **Use**: Scale sales and marketing

### 5. Series C+
- **Amount**: $30M+
- **Investors**: VCs, hedge funds
- **Valuation**: $200M+
- **Use**: Market dominance, expansion

## Financial Planning Best Practices

### 1. Build Multiple Scenarios
- Base case (most likely)
- Upside case (optimistic)
- Downside case (pessimistic)
- Plan for each

### 2. Update Regularly
- Monthly or quarterly updates
- Compare actual vs plan
- Adjust based on learnings

### 3. Focus on Cash
- Cash is king
- Monitor burn rate
- Plan runway carefully
- Know when to fundraise

### 4. Track Leading Indicators
- Pipeline (sales)
- Signups (SaaS)
- Usage (engagement)
- Leading > lagging

## Common Financial Mistakes
1. Ignoring unit economics
2. Underestimating costs
3. Overestimating revenue
4. Poor cash management
5. Not planning for downturns
6. Mixing personal and business finances
7. Not having financial visibility
