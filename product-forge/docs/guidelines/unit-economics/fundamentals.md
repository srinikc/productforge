# Unit Economics

## Overview
Unit economics measures the revenue and costs associated with a single unit of business (typically a customer) to understand profitability at the per-unit level.

## Key Unit Economics Metrics

### 1. CAC (Customer Acquisition Cost)
- **Definition**: Total cost to acquire one customer
- **Formula**: Total Sales & Marketing Spend / New Customers
- **Includes**: Ads, sales salaries, tools, content creation
- **Benchmarks**: Varies widely by industry
  - SaaS: $200-$1500
  - Consumer: $5-$50
  - Enterprise: $5000-$50000

### 2. LTV (Customer Lifetime Value)
- **Definition**: Total revenue from a customer over their lifetime
- **Formula**: ARPU × Gross Margin / Churn Rate
- **Benchmarks**:
  - SaaS: 3-7x annual contract value
  - Consumer: 2-5x first purchase

### 3. LTV/CAC Ratio
- **Definition**: LTV divided by CAC
- **Benchmarks**:
  - <1: Losing money on each customer
  - 1-3: Sustainable but tight
  - 3-5: Healthy
  - >5: Underinvesting in growth
- **Target**: 3:1 or higher

### 4. CAC Payback Period
- **Definition**: Months to recover CAC from gross profit
- **Formula**: CAC / (ARPU × Gross Margin)
- **Benchmarks**:
  - SaaS: <12 months
  - Consumer: <3 months
  - Enterprise: <24 months

### 5. Gross Margin
- **Definition**: Revenue minus cost of goods sold
- **Formula**: (Revenue - COGS) / Revenue
- **Benchmarks**:
  - SaaS: 70-90%
  - E-commerce: 20-50%
  - Hardware: 10-30%
  - Services: 30-50%

### 6. ARPU (Average Revenue Per User)
- **Definition**: Average revenue per customer
- **Formula**: Total Revenue / Number of Customers
- **Use cases**: Track over time, segment by user type

### 7. ARPPU (Average Revenue Per Paying User)
- **Definition**: Revenue per paying user (in freemium)
- **Formula**: Revenue / Paying Users
- **Use cases**: Freemium businesses

### 8. Contribution Margin
- **Definition**: Revenue minus variable costs
- **Formula**: Revenue - COGS - Variable Costs
- **Use cases**: Per-unit profitability

## SaaS-Specific Unit Economics

### Magic Number
- **Definition**: Sales efficiency metric
- **Formula**: (Current Quarter ARR - Previous Quarter ARR) × 4 / Previous Quarter S&M Spend
- **Benchmarks**:
  - <0.5: Inefficient
  - 0.5-0.75: Good
  - >0.75: Excellent

### Rule of 40
- **Definition**: Growth rate + profit margin should be >40%
- **Formula**: Revenue Growth % + Profit Margin %
- **Use cases**: SaaS health check

### Burn Multiple
- **Definition**: Capital efficiency
- **Formula**: Net Burn / Net New ARR
- **Benchmarks**:
  - <1: Excellent
  - 1-2: Good
  - >3: Concerning

## E-commerce Unit Economics

### Contribution Margin per Order
- **Formula**: Order Value - COGS - Shipping - Payment Fees - Returns
- **Must be positive for sustainable growth**

### First Purchase Economics
- Often loss-leading
- LTV comes from repeat purchases
- Measure payback period

## Marketplace Unit Economics

### Take Rate
- **Definition**: % of transaction value you keep
- **Formula**: Platform Revenue / GMV
- **Benchmarks**: 5-30% depending on category

### Liquidity Metrics
- Time to first transaction
- Fill rate (% of requests matched)
- Search-to-book conversion

## Improving Unit Economics

### Reduce CAC
- Improve conversion rates
- Better targeting
- Organic channels
- Referral programs
- Brand building

### Increase LTV
- Reduce churn
- Upsell/cross-sell
- Price increases
- Usage expansion
- Reactivation campaigns

### Improve Margins
- Reduce COGS
- Automate operations
- Negotiate better terms
- Scale efficiencies

## Unit Economics by Stage

### Pre-PMF
- Don't focus on unit economics
- Focus on retention and engagement
- Some CAC is okay for learning

### Early Stage
- CAC payback < 12 months
- LTV/CAC > 1.5
- Some inefficiency is okay

### Growth Stage
- LTV/CAC > 3
- Payback < 12 months
- Improving over time

### Scale Stage
- Optimized unit economics
- Defending and expanding
- New segments and products

## Common Unit Economics Mistakes
1. Ignoring customer support costs
2. Not accounting for refunds and chargebacks
3. Overlooking sales salaries in CAC
4. Optimizing LTV or CAC in isolation
5. Not measuring by segment
6. Ignoring payback period
7. Mixing gross and net metrics
