## 2. Requirements / acceptance criteria
- A subscriber receives events through inproc, webhook and notify/email transports with dispatcher-owned retry/DLQ.
- Webhook requests carry timestamp+body HMAC, event_id idempotency key, reject invalid signatures, and classify 4xx/409/429/5xx correctly.
- Notify always stores in-app notifications and sends email only for eligible priorities when SMTP is configured; missing SMTP does not fail in-app delivery.
- Transports enforce target/security policy, use one registry, emit observability, and are covered by unit plus dispatcher integration tests.
