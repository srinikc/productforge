## 2. Requirements / acceptance criteria
- fix-guard selects the newest review by recorded timestamp, not checkout mtime, and skips already-closed backlog items for CI enforcement.
- architect review and fix-guard share one deterministic review-selection helper, with regression tests covering both.
- Closed-item skipping and mtime fallback are explicit and observable, not silent gate bypasses.

## 3. Scope and non-goals
### In scope
- CI gate review selection
- closed-item skip
### Non-goals
- No change to review findings or dispositions.

## 4. Logic invariants and state model
- Review selection is deterministic: recorded at first, filesystem mtime only as a legacy fallback.
- Closed items cannot be completed again and must not block unrelated PRs.
- The same selector is used by fix-guard and architect review.
