## 2. Requirements / acceptance criteria
- AC-1: scripts/dev/deliver.py --item <ID> automates local feature branch delivery end-to-end in a single command.
- AC-2: Stages and commits unstaged Gate 2.5 review evidence and backlog completion items separately to comply with pre-commit hook constraints.
- AC-3: Runs scripts/dev/sync_develop.py and scripts/dev/wired_audit.py to ensure pre-merge compliance before branch checkout.
- AC-4: Performs controlled merge (--no-ff) into develop, pushes to origin/develop, stamps links.delivery on backlog item in DB and disk mirror.
- AC-5: scripts/dev/finalize_item.py provides --deliver flag delegating seamlessly to deliver.py.
