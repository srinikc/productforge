## 2. Requirements / acceptance criteria
- Given a backlog item with execution_mode='hil' (or unspecified), when gate_orchestrator.py executes, then it prompts and halts at Gate 1 and Gate 2 for human operator interaction.

- Given an item in auto mode after successful test execution, when Gate 2.5 and Gate 2 run, then finalize_item.py stamps AC verifications, candidate SHA, DoD evidence, Gate 2 approval (by='hil:auto'), and precheck fast tier passes cleanly.
