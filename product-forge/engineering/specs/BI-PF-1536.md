## 7. Functional requirements
### FR-1 — Deterministic review selection
- **priority:** P0
- **source:** BI-PF-1536
- **preconditions:** Review files exist.
- **trigger:** Selector runs.
- **inputs:** Item id, mode, reviews.
- **outputs:** Newest review by at.
- **errors:** Malformed review skipped.
- **auth:** CI tooling only.
- **idempotency:** Stable across runs.
- **data_rules:** mtime is fallback only.
- **edge_cases:** Z vs +00:00 timestamps.
- **acceptance:** Given two reviews When dates differ Then newest recorded at wins regardless of mtime.
- **verification:** test_fix_guard.py

### FR-2 — Skip closed items in fix-guard --all
- **priority:** P0
- **source:** BI-PF-1536
- **preconditions:** Item status is closed.
- **trigger:** fix-guard --all runs.
- **inputs:** All backlog items.
- **outputs:** Skipped count; no false gate failure.
- **errors:** Open items still enforced.
- **auth:** CI tooling only.
- **idempotency:** Stable.
- **data_rules:** Closed items already completed.
- **edge_cases:** Closed item has old major findings.
- **acceptance:** Given a completed item with old findings When fix-guard --all runs Then it is skipped and reported.
- **verification:** test_fix_guard.py
