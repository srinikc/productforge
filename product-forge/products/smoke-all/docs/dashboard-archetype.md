## Design Principles

- Enable data exploration
- Provide filters and date ranges
- Show statistical significance
- Support drill-down to raw data

## Things to Avoid

- Overwhelming with too many charts
- Missing data labels
- No clear narrative
- Charts that don't communicate insights
